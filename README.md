# Commercial Real Estate Market Research

Independent market briefs written from public brokerage data, as samples of CRE research and data storytelling.

## Briefs

| Market | Sector | Period | Report |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Metro Phoenix, AZ | Industrial | Q3 2026 | [Phoenix Industrial Market Brief](Phoenix_Industrial_Market_Brief_Q3_2026.pdf) |

**Phoenix highlights:** vacancy fell 250 bps year over year to 11.1% as 16.1M SF was absorbed over four quarters. The construction pipeline has rebounded above 17M SF, most of it speculative.

## Method
Quarterly series come from one brokerage (Lee & Associates) for consistency, since brokerages report vacancy differently (10.3%–11.5% for Phoenix in Q3 2026). Supporting figures come from Cushman & Wakefield and Kidder Mathews. Sources are linked in each brief.
